Who We Are

Lubbock's Local Biohazard Cleanup Professionals

We are a team of certified biohazard remediation specialists based right here in Lubbock, Texas. We serve our community during some of the most difficult moments families and property owners face.

Unlike large national call centers that route your call to whoever is available, we are locally based. When you call us, you reach someone who knows Lubbock, understands our community, and can be at your door quickly.

Every member of our team is trained in OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards, EPA biohazard disposal regulations, and Texas-specific requirements for biohazardous waste handling. We take our certifications seriously because your safety depends on it.
