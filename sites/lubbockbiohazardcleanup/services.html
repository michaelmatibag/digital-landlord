Crime Scene Cleanup

After law enforcement has completed their investigation, the property owner is responsible for cleanup. This isn't something that should be handled without professional equipment and training.

Our technicians safely remove blood, bodily fluids, and other biohazardous materials from the scene. We decontaminate all affected surfaces, eliminate odors, and restore the space to a safe, livable condition.

  • Complete biohazard removal and disposal
  • Surface decontamination and sanitization
  • Odor elimination using professional-grade equipment
  • Fingerprint powder and evidence chemical removal
  • Property restoration coordination if needed
Request Service

Why Professional Cleanup Matters

Blood and bodily fluids can contain dangerous pathogens including Hepatitis B & C, HIV, MRSA, and more. Household cleaning products cannot eliminate these threats. Improper cleanup exposes you and your family to serious health risks — and may leave your home with permanent damage and odors.

Insurance Tip

Crime scene cleanup is typically covered under homeowners insurance. Call us first — we'll help you navigate the claim.

Suicide Cleanup

Dealing with the aftermath of a suicide is one of the most painful experiences a family can face. You should not have to handle the physical cleanup yourself. That burden belongs with us.

Our team arrives in unmarked vehicles, works quietly and efficiently, and treats the space with complete respect. We work directly with your insurance company so your family has one less thing to worry about.

If you need support resources during this time, please reach out to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988.
